Every SME runs on small decisions.

Approve this expense? Who gets this ticket? Is this order risky?

Most of them live in someone's head: slow, inconsistent, impossible to audit.

I've been looking at AutoJEV, an open-source tool that turns a decision described in plain English into a tested decision engine:

1️⃣ Describe it: "should we auto-approve this expense claim, or send it to a manager?"
2️⃣ It weighs several designs and shows its scoring before it builds anything
3️⃣ You get plain, reviewable code with tests, and "not sure" always goes to a human

No black box. No guessing which approach fits. You can see why it chose what it did, and what would change its mind.

It runs inside Claude Code (or any agent via MCP), and it's MIT-licensed.

👉 github.com/exponen-agi/autojev

What's the one decision in your business you'd like to stop making by hand? 👇
